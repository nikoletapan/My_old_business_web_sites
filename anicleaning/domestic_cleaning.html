KITCHEN:

-clean oven/grill, hobs, fridge, microwave
-hand washing up and dry, load and empty
  dishwasher and put away when cycle complete
-empty and wash bin, replace bin liner
-clean and polish front of cupboards, cooker,
  washing machine, work surfaces, sinks, taps etc.
-clean walls, doors from stains and fingerprints
- hoover and wash floor.
BATHROOM:

-sanitize/disinfect lavatory bowl, bath/shower, sink,
  taps,
-replace towels, sanitary supplies, soap
-clean mirrors, windows, windowsills
-hoover and wash floor.
LIVING ROOM, BEDROOM:

-change bed linen,
-clean mirrors, windows, windowsills, skirting,
-remove marks from walls, light switches, doorhandles,
-hoover upholstery and floor and wash floor,
-dust/polish furniture, doors, top of doors, frames,
  lampshades, blinds.
